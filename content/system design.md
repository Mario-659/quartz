# framework

Structure of interview process.

1) **functional requirements**
	- what users can do
	- focus only on main points
2) **non-functional requirements**
	- CAP, scalability, compliance, environment constraints
3) **capacity estimation**
	- skip during initial phase, and do it on the go while designing specific components
4) **core entities**
	- start with only primary entities
5) choose **API or System Interface**
	- REST, GraphQL, RPC
6) (optional) **data flow sequence**
7) **high level design**
8) **deep dives**

General rules:
- time management - for mid level positions *high level design* is the priority
- deliver working design
- do not go too deep into specifics
